Hello!
My name is Nadya Izotova, I’m an illustrator currently based in Slovenia.

I love illustrating books and designing characters that feel warm, textured, and delightfully playful. My ideas are rooted in real life, capturing everyday moments and the small details that make art come alive. I work in Procreate, treating each book as a complete visual journey rather than individual pages.

Apart from illustration, my hobbies are yoga, plants, mountains, and vintage shopping.

For inquiries or to explore potential collaborations, please feel free to contact me!
